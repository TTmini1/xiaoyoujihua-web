A LITTLE CLARITY

Questions, answered.

Good tea should feel approachable.

Is this store accepting real orders?

Not yet. This is an independent demonstration website with two illustrative teas: Longjing green tea, 50g at HKD 168, and Tie Guan Yin oolong, 50g at HKD 128. Product details and AI-generated images are illustrative, not confirmed stock. There is no online payment or backend order system. Business details and sales terms must be confirmed before real selling begins.

Which tea should I start with?

If you enjoy a fresh, lighter cup, explore green tea. If you prefer a rounder, gently floral cup, explore oolong. The best starting point is your own taste.

Why are prices displayed in HKD?

Hong Kong dollars are used for the two demonstration prices. These are illustrative item prices, not a final order quote. Shipping, taxes, availability and any future payment arrangements must be confirmed by email.

What happens when I make an order enquiry?

Your shopping bag is saved in localStorage in this browser. The enquiry action prepares an email draft to tyminye@gmail.com in your email app. Nothing is sent to the owner until you review and send that draft yourself. Opening a draft does not place an order or guarantee a reply.

How should I store loose-leaf tea?

Keep tea in a sealed container away from heat, moisture, strong smells and direct sunlight. Follow the final product’s storage and best-before guidance.

Can I enquire about wholesale?

Yes. Use our wholesale form to prepare an email draft with your contact information, country and tea interests. You must send it from your email app. Minimum quantities, sample availability and lead times are not yet confirmed.

Do you ship to my country?

Shipping terms and delivery destinations are not yet configured. There is no worldwide delivery guarantee. Ask by email for destination availability and a quote confirming shipping charges, taxes and estimated timing before any future purchase.
