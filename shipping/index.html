Clear information, before your cup begins its journey.

This is a demonstration website.

There is no online payment or backend order system. The two teas and displayed item prices are illustrative. Real sales must wait until the owner confirms the products, business details and final policies.

Where we deliver

Shipping terms and delivery destinations are not yet configured. We do not guarantee worldwide coverage or delivery to any particular address. Ask by email whether your destination can be served before considering a future purchase.

Shipping costs & timing

Delivery charges, available shipping methods and estimated timing must be confirmed in an email quote. The local shopping bag displays demonstration item amounts only; it does not calculate shipping or a final payable total. No dispatch schedule or delivery date is currently promised.

Customs, duties & taxes

Shipping charges and taxes must be confirmed by email for the proposed destination. Any applicable import duties, customs requirements and responsibility for those charges must also be clarified in the quote before a future sale. The displayed demonstration prices do not establish whether taxes or duties are included.

Returns & damaged parcels

The return window, product eligibility, damaged-parcel procedure and refund terms are not yet configured. Final terms and any applicable customer rights must be provided before real selling begins.

Requesting information
