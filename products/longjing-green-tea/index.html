A little about this tea

Longjing is a Chinese green tea traditionally associated with Zhejiang. This sample illustrates a fresh, pan-fired style.

Make it your ritual

Start with 3g of tea per 200ml of water. Try 80°C for 2 minutes, then adjust to your taste. Re-steep and explore how the cup changes.
