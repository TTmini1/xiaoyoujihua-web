SHANHE brings the language of Chinese tea to everyday life. A gentle green tea in the morning. A rounded oolong when the afternoon asks you to pause. No elaborate ceremony required—just water, leaves, and a little attention.

Our demonstration collection introduces two classic styles: Longjing green tea and Tie Guan Yin oolong. Explore their differences, learn a simple way to brew them, and discover what feels right for you.
