Preparing your tea bag…

LESS RUSH. MORE RITUAL.

Make a little room
for a good cup.

No special expertise needed. Start simply, then let your taste guide you.

01

Choose your leaves

Start with around 3g of loose-leaf tea for a 200ml cup. Give the leaves room to open.

02

Let the water settle

For delicate green tea, try 80°C. For oolong, try 90°C. These are starting points, not strict rules.

03

Give it a moment

Try 2 minutes for green tea or 3 minutes for oolong. Taste, then shorten or lengthen your next brew.

04

Return to the leaves

Re-steep and notice how the character changes. A second cup can tell a different story.

Your ritual, your way.

Use fresh water and a comfortable cup. There is no single perfect way to enjoy tea.
